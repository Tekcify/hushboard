# Code of Conduct

Hushboard follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

## Our pledge

We, as members, contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Respect differing opinions and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what's best for the people who use Hushboard.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention or advances.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel run by the project) and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers in the [Tekcify organisation](https://github.com/tekcify) (for example by contacting a maintainer directly, or through a private security advisory if nothing else fits). All reports are reviewed promptly and confidentially.

Maintainers may take any action they find appropriate, from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban, following the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
